If a wallet shows as not available
  • Make sure the wallet has at least one card saved.
  • Don't use an incognito or private window — wallets are hidden there.
  • Chrome: Settings → Autofill and passwords → Payment methods → turn on Allow sites to check if you have payment methods saved.
  • Safari: Settings → Advanced → turn on Allow websites to check for Apple Pay and Apple Card.
  • Apple Pay only appears in Safari on Apple devices. Google Pay appears in Chrome and most other browsers, but not in Safari.
  • Both wallets are only offered in supported Google Pay countries and supported Apple Pay countries.
